# Positioning in r53

Table I compares the objects selected and questions addressed by anomaly extraction, PacketScore, a JA3 mitigation report and this evaluation. It is a map of approaches, not an experimental ranking.

The contribution evaluates when endpoint-relative fingerprint profiles permit selective filtering and how fleet activity, triggers and false alarms limit it. Claims about OWL, STIX or query compilation in older documentation describe implementation history; they are not the central contribution of r53.

The study includes modal, z-score, novelty and history-based comparisons. It does not benchmark a complete commercial WAF, nor establish superiority over per-fingerprint rate limits, selected-fingerprint challenges or prefix filters. The discussion identifies these missing comparisons.

The authoritative bibliography and associated claims are in [article.tex](../papers/http-session-noms/article.tex) and [references.bib](../papers/http-session-noms/references.bib).
