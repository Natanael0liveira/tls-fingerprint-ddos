# Methodology for r53

Start with [evaluation design](../docs/evaluation.md), [metrics](../docs/metrics.md) and [reproduction](../docs/reproduction.md). The frozen manuscript defines the current protocol and presentation.

The repository preserves the generated-campaign infrastructure and anonymized reductions. Historical sprints explored additional models and ontological representations; their results do not replace the r53 evaluation. The original pre-registered protocols and addenda remain in `sprint-6-noms/results/` with their original terminology and dates.

Current table exports are transcriptions of the frozen TeX. Independent checks against committed numerical reductions are explicitly counted by the audit. Missing underlying results must never be inferred or synthesized from the published table to claim reproduction.
